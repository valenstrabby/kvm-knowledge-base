
# The Golden Age of Arcades Games

## The Rise of Arcades 

Arcade games became an important part of video games history because they gave people at the time a new way to experience interactive entertainment.

### Why Arcades became Popular

Arcade provided games that were easy to understand but challenging to master.

- Players could compete for high scores.
- Arcades brought players together
- Games could be played without owning a console. 

> Arcade games helped introduce video games to a much wider audience.

## Important Arcade Games 

Some influential games include:

- Pac Man (1980)
- Space Invaders (1978)
- Donkey Kong (1981)
- Galaga (1981)
- Dance Dance Revolution (1998)
